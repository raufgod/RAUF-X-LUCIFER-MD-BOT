Cookie Policy

Short and honest: we only keep what is needed to make the site work.

Last updated: 11 November 2026· Applies to this website and the RAUF × LUCIFER MD BOT service

On this page1. What this page covers2. What we store3. Third parties and other loads4. Your choices5. Legal basis

1. What this page covers

This site uses a small number of cookies and browser-storage items. We use no advertising cookies and no analytics or tracking cookies; we checked the website code for this. "Browser storage" means small items kept in your own browser (localStorage or sessionStorage), which we treat like cookies in this policy. Items are split into essential (the site cannot work without them) and optional preferences (only stored if you allow them).

3. Third parties and other loads

Fonts load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com); Google receives your IP address when your browser requests them, and we do not add cookies for it. The hosting or tunnel provider that carries the site (for example ngrok or a CDN) may set strictly technical cookies or show its own notices for security and routing; we do not control them. We found no other external scripts, analytics or advertising on the site pages.

4. Your choices

  • Use Privacy choices in the footer to see your current choice, switch optional preferences on or off, or withdraw all consent. Withdrawing clears optional storage and the visitor ID, and the first-visit notice appears again.
  • Essential items cannot be switched off inside the site because the site would stop working.
  • You can also delete everything in your browser settings (clear site data); the site will then ask again.

5. Legal basis

Essential items are used because the site cannot work without them. Optional preferences rely on your consent, which you can change at any time. This follows the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (parts of which apply in stages; see the Privacy Policy).
