1. Who we are

RAUF × LUCIFER MD BOT is run by Team RAUF × LUCIFER (developer: RAUF) ("we", "us"). For the data described here we decide why and how it is processed, so we act as the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). Contact: e-mail our support e-mail, Telegram @Next_DYS or WhatsApp +91 91039 02586.

2. Legal framework and consent

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you accept the first-visit notice, enter your number to link it, or register for the voice walkthrough, and for limited uses the law allows. Each consent is for the purposes in this policy. You can withdraw it at any time (see sections 9 and 10); withdrawing does not undo what was done before, but the bot cannot work for you afterwards.

Status of the law: the DPDP Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 13 November 2025) are coming into force in stages. The Rules on notice, consent, data-principal rights, security safeguards and breach reporting apply from 13 May 2027. We already follow these principles and offer the rights below voluntarily; some details may change when the rules become enforceable. The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the IT Rules, 2021 also apply. This policy is not legal advice.

4. AI features and third-party processing

  • What is sent: when you use .gpt, .gemini, .deepseek, .asyntai, .talkai, .notegpt, .zonerai, .pollinationai, .mathgpt or the website AI Support, your text and the recent conversation (for context) are sent to an AI API service (eliteprotech-apis.zone.id), which passes it to the chosen AI model. The text travels inside the web address (URL) of the request, so it can also appear in the logs of that service or of networks on the way. Please do not type personal or confidential details into AI features. .imagine sends your picture description to an image service. The TechX commands (for example .bard, .flux, .currency, .aiodl, .shortlink) send what you type, or the link you give, to techxkamran.onrender.com; it is a third-party service outside our control, so its retention and privacy practices are not covered by this policy. Use these commands only for non-personal content. Do not send passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive information to any AI feature.
  • Voice to AI (optional): if you add your own Groq key, your voice note is sent to Groq for transcription and the answer is generated by Groq, then spoken back with a Microsoft Edge text-to-speech voice. Without a key no voice message is processed.
  • Memory: each AI remembers the last 20 messages of your chat with it. This history lives in server memory only, per chat (WhatsApp) or per visitor ID (website), expires after about 6 hours without use and is lost when the bot restarts. A separate file keeps only message IDs and the name of the AI that wrote each reply for up to 7 days so that replying to an answer works; it does not contain message text.
  • Clearing: send .gpt clear (same for the other AI commands) or use Clear this chat on the AI Support page. Withdrawing consent through Privacy choices also clears your website chat memory.
  • Providers: we do not control how these providers store or use what you send, and we have not verified their retention. Treat anything you send to an AI feature as shared with them.
  • Remove your Groq key: send .aikey remove in WhatsApp.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. Data may reach these parties only as needed to run the service:

  • WhatsApp / Meta and Telegram, because the bot works through their platforms.
  • AI, voice, media, download and other API providers, when you use a command that needs one: the text, link or file you send to that command is passed to the provider.
  • Hosting and tunnel providers (for example the server host and the tunnel or CDN service that carries the site): web traffic passes through them.
  • Google Fonts: your browser fetches fonts from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Authorities, if required by Indian law, a court order, or to protect users and the service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Linked session and bot settings: until you unlink or remove the session, or ask us to delete them.
  • Pairing attempts that never finish: removed on the next attempt or restart.
  • Voice walkthrough record: until you ask us to delete it. A STOP request keeps the number on a do-not-contact list so we can honour it.
  • Custom bot profile and logo: until you ask us to delete them.
  • AI chat memory: up to about 6 hours (server memory); reply-tracking IDs: up to 7 days.
  • Backups: the automatic backup (every 3 hours by default) holds only small user-data files: settings, warnings, bans, group data and the owner map of linked numbers and Telegram IDs. It does not contain WhatsApp login credentials or keys, AI keys, the .env file, code or media. Only the newest backups are kept (100 by default), so older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Rate-limit and IP data: memory only, cleared within minutes or on restart.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards: the admin panel opens only through a private link generated at start-up; session and key files are kept on the server and not in public website folders; edit keys are stored as salted hashes; login attempts and registration are rate limited; phone numbers are masked in server logs; and the website uses HTTPS through the hosting provider. No system is 100% secure. Please protect your phone, never share pairing codes or edit keys, and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

8. Age limit

The service is only for people who are 18 years or older. You confirm this when you accept the first-visit notice, and we do not knowingly process personal data of children. A tick box cannot prove age, so if you believe someone under 18 has used the service, tell us and we will delete the data.

9. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask to know what data we hold about you and why, correct or update it, erase it, withdraw consent, get grievance redressal, and nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act. To use a right, contact us using the details in section 14 from the number or Telegram account the request is about, so we can check it is you. We will answer within a reasonable time.

10. Deleting your data

  • Unlink the bot: WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → remove it. Then ask us to delete the stored session, settings and any custom bot or walkthrough record for your number.
  • This website: use Privacy choices in the footer. It shows your current choice, lets you turn optional preferences on or off, or withdraw all consent (which also clears optional storage, the visitor ID and your website chat memory). You can also clear site data in your browser.
  • Custom bot edit key: we cannot recover a lost key. Contact us if you want the custom bot profile removed.
  • AI chat: send .gpt clear or use Clear this chat; remove a Groq key with .aikey remove.
  • Voice walkthrough: send STOP to the assistant to end messages.

11. Data breach

If a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, describing what happened and what you can do.

12. Data outside India

The AI, hosting and other providers listed above may store or process data outside India. We send data to them only to the extent needed for the feature you use.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For material changes we will ask for your consent again; the consent notice on this website carries a version number for that reason.

14. Grievance contact

Grievance contact: Team RAUF × LUCIFER, e-mail our support e-mail, Telegram @Next_DYS or WhatsApp +91 91039 02586. A named Grievance Officer has not been appointed or published yet; until then every complaint goes to this contact. We aim to acknowledge a complaint within 24 hours and to resolve it within 15 days, the timelines used in the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operating for your case.
