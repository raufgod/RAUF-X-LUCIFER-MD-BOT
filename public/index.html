What is RAUF × LUCIFER MD BOT?

RAUF × LUCIFER MD BOT is a free, multi-device WhatsApp bot built with Node.js and Baileys. It has more than 600 commands — music and video downloads, an AI assistant, stickers and media tools, group administration, anime and fun commands and everyday utilities. You link your own WhatsApp number on this website with an 8-character pairing code (no QR scan), then send commands such as .menu in WhatsApp. A companion Telegram bot can also create sessions. This website is the control and information site; it is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
